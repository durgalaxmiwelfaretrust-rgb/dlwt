About Durgalaxmi Welfare Trust

A public welfare initiative focused on education, environment and community development.

Our story

Durgalaxmi Welfare Trust (DLWT) is committed to creating opportunities and supporting initiatives that contribute to a more educated, sustainable and inclusive society. Through community-focused programmes, the Trust aims to make learning resources more accessible and encourage responsible care for the environment.

Our vision

A more equitable and informed society

A future where people can access learning opportunities, participate in community life and live in a cleaner, healthier environment.

Our mission

Turn commitment into action

Develop and support practical programmes in education, libraries, environmental protection and community welfare.

Our objectives

  • Support education for underprivileged girls and children.
  • Promote reading, knowledge access and village libraries.
  • Encourage tree plantation and environmental awareness.
  • Support community development and educational infrastructure.
  • Promote compassion and responsible animal welfare.
  • Encourage research, scholarship and academic collaboration.
