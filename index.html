WHO WE ARE

About Durgalaxmi Welfare Trust

Durgalaxmi Welfare Trust (DLWT) is committed to advancing education, expanding access to knowledge, supporting environmental care and strengthening communities.

Our initiatives focus on practical opportunities for learning and social development, including support for girls’ education, village libraries, tree plantation and community welfare activities.

OUR FOCUS AREAS

Initiatives that create lasting value

Our work is guided by the belief that education, environmental responsibility and community participation can help create stronger futures.

📚

Every Village a Library

A knowledge-access campaign to encourage libraries and reading opportunities in villages and underserved communities.

🎓

Girls’ Education

Supporting the education of underprivileged girls, including assistance towards annual school fees.

🌱

Environment & Tree Plantation

Promoting greener communities through tree-planting and environmental awareness activities.

Education & Schools

Working towards educational opportunities and the development of learning spaces.

🐄
